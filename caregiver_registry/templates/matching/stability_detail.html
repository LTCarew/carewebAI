📖 What do these terms mean? (click to expand)
Relationship Signals
Schedule Consistency
How reliably scheduled sessions have been happening, based on the Reliability & Consistency ratings submitted by both the client and careworker after sessions.
Travel Burden
The driving distance between the careworker and client. This is the only signal not based on session ratings — it is a fixed geographic measurement calculated when the match was created.
Access Alignment
How well the careworker's approach fits the client's needs and treats them with respect, based on Care Fit & Respect ratings.
Care Continuity
Overall relationship stability, calculated from the combined average of all four rating categories. Lower scores may indicate that friction is building up in the relationship.
Support Flags
Whether the workload feels sustainable and appropriately supported for everyone involved, based on Workload & Support Balance ratings. This signal is also automatically set to "Immediate review recommended" if a staff member has manually flagged this relationship.
Session Rating Metrics (1–10 scale)
Care Fit & Respect
"Does this careworker's approach fit the client's needs and treat them with dignity and respect?"
Communication
"How well do the careworker and client communicate and coordinate around scheduling, needs, and expectations?"
Reliability
"Attendance, punctuality, and follow-through on scheduled sessions."
Workload Balance
"Does this care arrangement feel sustainable and appropriately supported for everyone involved?" Low scores here often signal caregiver burnout or a mismatch between the tasks agreed to and what is actually happening.
